Why it matters: what happens without one
Nov 2026 · Researchers, Crissy Field 24,664

Lovable projects returned data with no login

Researchers checked 159,041 Lovable projects. Of the 34,545 using Supabase, 24,664 gave out database records to anyone. The worst exposed passport numbers, ticket and order payment details, and over 200,000 chat messages. Lovable itself wasn't hacked: the apps were missing database access rules.

Source: Cybernews
Feb 2026 · One app 18,697

User records exposed by a single Lovable app

A researcher found one Lovable-built app exposing 18,697 user records, including 14,928 unique email addresses. The same flaws reportedly let anyone send bulk emails as the app and delete user accounts, without logging in.

Source: The Register
May 2025 · CVE-2025-48757 170

Showcase apps with open databases

A scan of 1,645 apps from Lovable's own showcase found 170 with weak database rules: 303 open endpoints leaking home addresses, personal debt amounts, API keys and other personal data. It was serious enough to get its own CVE.

Source: Superblocks
