# About Me

I'm Amaechina Ikechukwu (Ikay), a software engineer and technical instructor based in Nigeria. I build web and mobile products end to end, from the backend logic to the interface people actually touch, and I teach the same skills to the next generation of developers.

I started out self-taught, learning by building real things and breaking them until they worked. I later formalized that foundation with a backend engineering certification from Vephla. Today I split my time between running Robinson March Software Systems, my software consultancy, and teaching the Frontend Engineering Program at RAD5 Tech Hub in Aba, where I train students in JavaScript, React, and the full frontend toolchain.

My work spans frontend engineering (React), mobile development (React Native and Expo), and backend systems (Bun, Express, PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM, and Firebase). I've integrated payment systems like Paystack and Flutterwave into production apps, and I regularly take on client projects that need a developer who can own a problem from database schema to shipped screen.

Whether I'm building a product from scratch, maintaining a client's existing platform, or explaining the event loop to a room of students, the goal is the same: build things that work, and make sure they still work six months from now.

## Vigil Med
**Medication reminder and health tracking app**

Vigil Med helps people stay on top of their medications and health routines. It combines full-screen dose alarms, flexible prescription schedules, and drug and food interaction checks (sourced from NIH DailyMed, MedlinePlus, and NCCIH) with adherence tracking, vitals logging, and doctor-shareable health summaries. All data stays on-device by default, and an account is optional. A free plan covers the essentials, with Vigil Premium unlocking unlimited medications, vitals logging, and appointment tracking.

[Visit Vigil Med →](https://vigilmed.health/)
